# Original mascots and a necessary counterweight

This candidate integrates the twelve offline Blender expressions reviewed from artifact 10695833011, run 35732939923, source 78c361d. The atlas is committed in the repository with full provenance; no live model API, Tripo balance or future artifact download is needed by the APK. Runtime validates its hash and decodes one 768x576 alpha atlas per process. A physical radius r uses a sprite square of side 3.6r and a fixed centre, preserving the body's collision alignment. Leaves and ears remain decorative.

Level 6 is no longer a recovery funnel with a decorative seesaw. A falling counterweight must tip a bridge so all three friends reach a protected landing. Removing the weight, lever, landing floor or landing guard independently prevents success. The production physics engine is unchanged.

Before committing, executed 160 offline Kotlin/JVM simulations using the actual physics sources and lightweight Android value adapters: all choices, repeated frame schedules, a 5x5 grid of initial offsets and four mechanism-removal counterfactuals. All passed. A higher falling weight and a double-spring design were REJECTED after stability/collision failures; they are not in the game. These simulations are not Android recordings and do not certify fun or bug freedom.

The new art and level require a fresh actual Android run and frame review before approval. Eight boards remain recovery funnels; the final difficulty curve, small-screen/font review and controlled performance measurement are still pending. Retain all lifecycle assertions and separate test recordings. Main stays untouched.
